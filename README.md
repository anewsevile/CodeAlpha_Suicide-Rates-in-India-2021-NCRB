## 📌 Overview

This project analyzes the **cause-wise distribution of suicides in India for the year 2021** using data from the **National Crime Records Bureau (NCRB)**. The goal is to transform raw data into meaningful visual insights that highlight patterns across causes, gender, and age groups.

---

## 📊 Visualizations

| Chart | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 🔴 Top 10 Causes | Horizontal bar chart of the most common causes |
| 🔵 Gender Split | Male vs Female comparison across top 8 causes |
| 🟣 Age Group Donut | Distribution of suicides across age groups |
| 🟠 Gender Pie | Overall male vs female percentage breakdown |

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- **Family Problems** is the #1 cause with over **54,000 cases** — nearly 3× the second highest
- **Males account for ~73%** of all suicide deaths in 2021
- The **30–45 age group** is the most vulnerable demographic
- **Mental Illness & Drug/Alcohol Addiction** are major contributors highlighting a public health concern
- **Failure in Examinations** spikes in the **under-18** group — a serious student mental health issue
